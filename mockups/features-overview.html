01 · The core idea

Computed inventory, never stored counts

Nothing ticks a "remaining pills" counter in the background. Each supplement stores a quantity anchor — an on-hand snapshot and timestamp — and current counts are derived by subtracting consumption since then, oldest bottle first (docs/adr/0001).

⚓ Quantity anchor

The stored truth: pills on hand at a moment, distributed across bottles oldest-first. Any real-world change — recount, dosage edit, new bottle, taker added — triggers a re-anchor, so new rates apply only going forward.

🍾 Bottle ledger

Each bottle is its own row with label count and price paid. The open bottle is the oldest not yet drained; sealed spares queue behind it. When it empties, the next auto-rolls open — FIFO falls out of the math, nothing to trigger.

📉 Run-out forecast

daysLeft = on hand ÷ rate, where rate is Σ(takers' pills-per-week) ÷ 7. Thresholds drive the status pills above: critical ≤ 7 days, low ≤ 25, on-track ≤ 60, stocked beyond.

02 · Interchangeable brands

Groups: pooled FIFO or manual-open

A Group links ≥2 interchangeable brands that fill the same role ("Fish Oil"). It owns one combined forecast, cost roll-up, per-person dosage, and drain queue — and auto-dissolves back to a solo supplement if unlinked down to one member.

⏭️ Default: group queue (pooled FIFO)

  • All bottles across brands drain oldest-purchase-first, currently-open at the front.
  • A newly bought bottle lands by purchase date — newest at the back, even of an earlier brand.
  • Dosage is set once per person on the group; a brand may carry a per-brand override for half-strength products (making the rate piecewise).

🖐️ Opt-in: manual-open (ADR-0010)

  • User marks bottles "on the counter"; the dosage splits equally across activated bottles.
  • Unpinned bottles freeze; the next enters rotation only when activated — no auto-fill.
  • Solo supplements always stay single-bottle FIFO.
03 · Purchasing

The Restock Planner

One active worksheet per household, user-curated only — the run-out forecast orders candidates and badges urgency, but never adds items itself. Persists until its items are purchased or removed; no explicit start/end action (docs/adr/0006, 0009).

1 · Pick subjectsChoose from supplements ordered by run-out; urgency highlighted within the forecast window (default 30 days).
→
2 · Compare candidatesDurable product options per subject — retailer, URL, label, count. One candidate selected per item per cycle; $/pill comparison when count is known.
→
3 · Retailer basketsDerived grouping: subtotal, free-shipping threshold status, applied shipping, all-in total. Nudges flag the cheapest complete basket.
→
4 · Mark as purchasedPer retailer order, with confirmation letting actuals differ from plan. Lands via match-or-add to a supplement; saved purchase links update automatically.

🎯 Coverage target

Household knob (default 90 days) driving recommended quantity: enough bottles that on-hand covers the target at the current rate. Always editable before buying.

🔗 Saved links vs candidates

A saved purchase link remembers where you'd rebuy a supplement at a retailer; it may seed candidates but never appears as a comparison option itself.

🔀 Substitutes welcome

Candidates aren't locked to stocked brands. Buying a different brand creates a new supplement — with an offer (user decides) to form a group.

04 · Who takes what

People, dosages & pauses

Each person takes supplements at pills per week — typed per-day, per-week, or by weekday, all reducing to a weekly total. Consumption rate is a smooth daily average, not tied to weekdays.

⏸️ Pause & resume

A dosage can be paused (optionally until a date) without deleting it. Paused dosages stay visible but drop out of rate, forecasts, and costs (docs/adr/0008). Dosage events record baseline / change / pause / resume history.

🧑 Disable vs delete

Disabling a person soft-pauses their whole regimen — reversible, blast radius zero. Deleting fronts a confirm offering Disable instead (docs/adr/0003). Each person's page expands into their full regimen for inline edits.

🎨 Household-scoped data

Everything lives under one household (e.g. "Mark & Lori") with real auth and membership checks via convex/authz.ts. Convex queries push changes to every signed-in member instantly.

05 · Money

Spend rate vs lifetime spent

💸 Spend rate — forward-looking

Consumption rate × cost-per-pill of the currently open bottle, shown per person and rolled up per household in day / week / month windows. Steps up as each pricier bottle rolls open (FIFO cost, docs/adr/0002).

🧾 Lifetime spent — historical

The sum of bottle prices ever logged — money actually paid, including bottles now empty. Attributable per person via dosage share. Insights labels historical intake as estimated planned intake, never claiming reconstructed consumption cost.

06 · Planning hub

Insights reports

Opens on KPI cards with no report pre-selected. Reports share consistent filters, empty states, and caveats (specs/insights-reports/).

👤 Cost by person

Current-rate cost allocated to each person by dosage share, drillable down to person × supplement detail rows.

📅 Planned intake over time

Estimated planned intake reconstructed from dosage-event history — explicitly labelled estimated, with a caveat banner.

💊 Nutrients & inventory

Nutrient compare/detail from label facts (DSLD lookup), plus inventory/run-out reporting cards.
